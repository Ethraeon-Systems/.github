# Contributor Code of Conduct

ETHRAEON Systems adopts the Contributor Covenant 2.1 as the baseline standard for community conduct across public repositories, discussions, and issues under this organization.

## Standard

We commit to a respectful, professional, and inclusive environment. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sexualized content, and doxing are not tolerated in any public ETHRAEON Systems channel.

## Scope

This standard applies to public repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any officially designated ETHRAEON Systems community channel.

## Enforcement

Reports of unacceptable behavior may be sent to jason@ethraeon.systems. Reports are reviewed by the CEO under sovereign authority. Consequences may include removal of comments, revocation of contribution privileges, and reporting to GitHub Trust and Safety.
