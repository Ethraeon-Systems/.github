# Security Policy

ETHRAEON Systems treats security as a first-class engineering property. This document describes how to disclose vulnerabilities in ETHRAEON Systems public artifacts.

## Supported surfaces

Public repositories under the ETHRAEON-Systems GitHub organization and the deployed public surfaces at https://ethraeon.ai and sibling domains.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Send disclosure to: jason@ethraeon.systems

Include: affected surface or repository, reproduction steps, observed behavior, expected behavior, and your contact information.

Acknowledgment: within 5 business days.
Triage window: 30 days.
Disclosure coordination: 90 days from acknowledgment, adjustable by mutual agreement for complex issues.

## Scope

In scope: ETHRAEON Systems owned repositories and public deployed surfaces.
Out of scope: third-party dependencies, hosted infrastructure providers (Cloudflare, GitHub, VPS providers), social engineering, physical attacks.

## Certification posture

ETHRAEON Systems is GitHub Enterprise verified with self-certified operational standards. SOC 2 Type II preparation is self-certified pending Q3 2026. Independent third-party audit is not claimed at this date. ISO 42001 readiness posture is maintained; certification is not claimed.

All security claims are verifiable against the ETHRAEON evidence chain rooted at 687e0c0f under CEO sovereign authority (S. Jason Prohaska).

## Safe harbor

Good-faith security research conducted within scope and without disruption to production systems will not be pursued as a violation of terms.
